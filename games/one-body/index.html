ONE BODY

1–6 players. One wobbly creature. Each of you controls a limb (bots run the rest), and one limb is secretly trying to make it fall over.
or join a friend
🌐 Online rooms and Quick Play are on rank-game.com. Play with friends there!
How to play
🦵 Everyone controls one limb of the same creature: a leg, an arm, the head or the tail. Bots fill the rest, and playing solo you get both legs.
⬅️ ➡️ swing your limb · Space does its move (legs stomp, arms grab, tail boings, head honks).
🎂 Walk the creature to the cake before time runs out.
😈 One limb is the traitor. It can fake hiccups and give other limbs cramps. After every fall, watch the instant replay to see who moved.
🚨 Call a meeting and vote. The limb with the most votes gets pulled off. Pull off the traitor and you win!
