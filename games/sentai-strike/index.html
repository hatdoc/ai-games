SENTAI STRIKE

Three heroes. One city. Protect the people, beat the monster, then fight it again in a giant robot.

❓ How to play

Move ←→ · Jump ↑ · Guard hold ↓ · Dash double-tap ←/→
Attack Z (keep pressing for a combo, in the air for a dive kick)
Special X (uses 1 bar of the yellow gauge, which fills as you hit things):
  X energy blast · ↑+X rising uppercut · ↓+X ground slam
  →→X rushing strike · ←←X counter stance (get hit and strike back)
Swap hero C. Swap right after a hit for a TAG COMBO dropkick. Heroes on the bench slowly heal.
WASD, J/K/L and a gamepad work too. On phones use the buttons.
1️⃣ Protect the city. Grunts grab fleeing people and carry them off: knock the grunt down to free them. The blue gauge fills as you hold out.
2️⃣ Beat the boss. Watch for the red glow: that attack is coming. Launchers (↑+X, ↓+X, tag kicks) break shells and guards.
3️⃣ Team finisher! Press X when the boss is down.
4️⃣ Giant robot battle. Z attack, hold X to charge a big hit, ↓ guard, ↑ thruster hop, C switches pilot (Red = sword, Blue = blasters, Yellow = drill fist).
  🏢 If you dodge an attack, the building behind you gets hit. Guard to save the city!
