MEMORY DUEL

You hide your cards. They hide theirs. Remember where yours are, then find their matching card before they find yours.
How to play 1️⃣ Every pair is split: blue cards are yours, red cards are theirs. You secretly place yours on your side (the bottom half), they place theirs on top. 2️⃣ On your turn, flip one of your cards, then guess which of their cards matches it. 3️⃣ Match = points and another turn (streaks multiply). Miss = their turn, and you both saw what flipped. 🌀 On Hard and above, the board moves between turns, always with a warning. Track it! 🎒 One item per turn: Preview, Double Points, Peek, Extra Guess, Freeze, Scanner, Score Steal, Rewind (undo the board's last move).
