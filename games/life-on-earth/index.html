Life on Earth

Billions of beginnings.
Countless ways to live.

You don't choose the life you're given. You are born as a human, a dog, an elephant, a penguin or something stranger, then discover what you can make of it, from first breath to last.

A human life takes about 10 to 15 minutes at normal pace: every year is told, and you choose at the moments that matter. Pause, slow down or speed up any time. Animal lives are shorter.
Share a seed and a friend is born into the same starting life.

Auto is off by default. When it is on, it follows this creature's temperament; irreversible choices always wait for you unless you tick this.
