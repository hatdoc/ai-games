How to play

Guess the hidden word. After each guess the tiles show how close you were:

GMint: right letter, right spot.
RAmber: in the word, wrong spot.
XSlate: not in the word.

Difficulties: Easy 4 letters / 7 tries · Normal 5 / 6 · Hard 6 letters, and you must keep greens and use ambers · Expert 7, and ambers can't go back where they were ruled out · Brutal 8 letters with 60 seconds per guess.

Hints: 📍 Pin shows one letter in place, 🧹 Sweep greys out 3 letters. They cost points in scored modes.

Fib mode: in every row exactly one tile shows the wrong colour (never on a correct answer). Long-press a tile to mark it as a liar 🤥.

Daily scores go on the leaderboard when you're signed in on rank-game.com. Invalid words never cost a try.
