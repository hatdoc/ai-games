LINE MAFIA

Everyone draws the secret word, one line at a time.
One of you has no idea what it is.
or join friends with a code
How to play
🎯 Everyone sees the category. Everyone except the mafia also sees the secret word.
✏️ Take turns drawing one line each on a shared page, a few laps around the table.
🕵️ The mafia has to fake it: draw something that fits without giving themselves away.
🗳️ Then vote. Catch the mafia and the town wins, unless the mafia guesses the word.
👥 3–10 players. Share the room code with friends, hit Quick Play, or practice against 4 computer players.
